## Executive Summary

This visual direction defines the complete aesthetic language for Jatin's portfolio—a dark, minimal, energetic system that lets work speak while maintaining a distinctive personality. Think **"Digital Studio with Door Open"**: refined darkness punctuated by warm orange energy, inviting yet focused, with typography and whitespace doing the heavy lifting.

**Design Philosophy**: Impact First, Craft Always, Personality Subtly

---

## Aesthetic Overview

### Aesthetic Keywords

**Minimal Energy** - **Dark Refined** - **Warmly Technical** - **Confident Quiet**

### Visual Mood (30-Second Description)

Walking into a designer's studio at night—the door is open, warm orange light spilling out invitingly. The space is dark but not cold, focused but not closed off. Clean surfaces, work pinned to walls with care, tools within reach. Everything intentional, nothing arbitrary. Professional craft with human warmth. Technical precision with creative soul. You're welcome to look around.

**Feeling**: You've discovered something worth your time. Confident without shouting, sophisticated without pretension, energetic without chaos, inviting without desperation.

### Reference Era/Style

**Primary**: Neo-Minimalism (2024-2026) - Bold choices, restrained execution, high contrast
**Secondary**: Editorial Digital (Print meets Web) - Typography-driven, spatial awareness
**Tertiary**: Dark Mode Excellence (Gaming/SaaS aesthetic) - Intentional darkness as brand expression

**NOT**: Corporate minimalism (too cold), Brutalism (too aggressive), Maximalism (too busy)

## Core Design Principles

These 7 principles guide every visual decision:

### 1. "Orange is Energy, Not Decoration"

Orange (#FFAB00) appears sparingly and strategically. It's your signature color—use it where it matters most:
- Primary CTAs that demand action
- Hero typography that establishes personality
- Hover states that reward interaction
- Signature elements (footer, personal brand)

**NOT**: Random orange splashes, orange everywhere, orange for orange's sake

**Test**: Remove all orange. If the design feels complete, you used it wrong. If it feels lifeless, you used it right.

### 2. "Darkness Creates Focus"

Pure black (#0A0A0A) backgrounds aren't edgy—they're functional. Dark recedes, letting work and orange accents command attention. High contrast creates hierarchy instantly.

**Approach**:
- Background: True dark (#0A0A0A, not gray)
- Cards: Elevated dark (#161A22)
- Text: High contrast white (#F5F5F5) for headings
- Borders: Subtle gray (#1F2937) for structure

**Test**: Squint at the design. Clear hierarchy visible? Good. Everything equally visible? Too noisy.

### 3. "Typography is Structure"

Cabinet Grotesk + Inter aren't just fonts—they're the visual scaffolding. Large, confident headings create rhythm. Body text fades into readability.

**Hierarchy**:
- **Hero (76px)**: Statement, personality, memorability
- **H1 (57px)**: Section ownership, clear boundaries
- **H2 (43px)**: Subsection structure
- **Body (18px)**: Effortless reading, neutral presence

**Test**: Hide all images. Can you still navigate by typography alone? Yes = good hierarchy.

### 4. "Whitespace is a Design Element"

Empty space isn't wasted space—it's breathing room, focus, luxury. Generous padding, single-column focus, unhurried rhythm.

**Spacing**:
- Mobile: 16-24px padding (tight but breathable)
- Desktop: 48-64px padding (spacious, confident)
- Between sections: 96px+ (clear boundaries)

**Test**: If it feels cramped, add 50% more whitespace. If it feels sparse, you're probably right.

### 5. "Motion Enhances, Never Distracts"

Animations are functional: they provide feedback, guide attention, reward interaction. Not decorative fireworks.

**Duration**:
- Micro-interactions: 200ms (hover, focus)
- State changes: 300ms (card hover, button press)
- Page transitions: 500ms (smooth, never jarring)

**Test**: Disable all animations. Is the site still usable? Yes = good motion design.

### 6. "Warmth Through Details"

Small human touches soften technical precision. Noise texture adds analog warmth to pure digital. Rounded corners (not sharp edges) feel approachable. Ms Madi signature adds personal touch. Orange glows create warmth on cold black.

**Details that humanize**:
- 3% noise texture overlay (analog feel)
- 8-12px border radius (friendly, not harsh)
- Ms Madi handwritten signature in footer
- Orange warmth vs cold blue (deliberately warm accent choice)
- Micro-interactions reward engagement (button lifts, card glows)

**NOT**: Cold corporate blue, sharp 0px corners, sterile perfection, robotic uniformity

**Test**: Does the site feel human or machine-made? If it feels warm despite darkness, you succeeded.

### 7. "Open by Design"

Visual language invites partnership without demanding it. Contact paths are clear but not pushy. Collaborative projects show team credits. CTA language invites ("Let's Talk") rather than begs ("Hire Me").

**Openness signals**:
- "Digital Studio with **Door Open**" metaphor (not closed/locked)
- Contact section as invitation, not transaction
- Collaborative project cards credit partners visibly
- CTA buttons say "Let's Talk" (collaborative) not "Hire Me" (desperate)
- Orange glow as "welcoming light from doorway"

**NOT**: Desperate pleas, hidden contact info, solo-only narrative, closed-off fortress

**Test**: Does a potential collaborator feel invited to reach out? Or does it feel like a locked gallery?

## Visual Elements

### Shapes & Forms

**Border Radius**:
- Cards: 12px (rounded but not bubbly)
- Buttons: 8px (friendly but professional)
- Images: 8px (subtle rounding)
- Small elements (badges, tags): 6px
- NO: Sharp 0px (too harsh), Full rounds (too playful)

**Geometric Language**:
- Rectangles and squares (stability, professionalism)
- Occasional circles (badges, avatars, decorative accents)
- NO complex shapes, no organic blobs, no polygons

**Decorative Shapes**:
- Subtle circular gradient glows (orange, low opacity)
- Thin horizontal lines as section dividers
- Grid patterns (code editor aesthetic, low contrast)

### Textures & Surfaces

**Primary Approach**: Smooth and flat (modern, digital)

**Subtle Additions**:
- **Noise texture**: 2-3% opacity film grain on dark backgrounds (adds warmth, reduces "flat" digital feel)
- **Gradients**: Mesh gradients for backgrounds (orange → darker orange, subtle)
- **Glass effects**: NONE (too trendy, dated quickly)
- **Material textures**: NONE (paper, fabric don't fit digital brand)

**Card Treatment**:
- Flat solid color (#161A22)
- Subtle border (#1F2937)
- NO: Drop shadows, bevels, embossing

### Depth & Dimension

**Approach**: Layered 2D (not flat, not 3D)

**Depth Techniques**:
1. **Subtle borders**: 1px borders create elevation without shadows
2. **Color shifts**: Elevated surfaces are slightly lighter (#161A22 vs #0A0A0A)
3. **Glow effects**: Orange glows on hover create depth
4. **Stacking**: Clear z-axis (background → cards → buttons)

**NO 3D Elements**:
- No Three.js 3D scenes (too complex for this brand)
- No isometric illustrations (doesn't fit minimal aesthetic)
- No CSS 3D transforms (unnecessary complexity)

**Shadows**: NONE on dark backgrounds (don't show). Use glows instead.

### Whitespace Approach

**Philosophy**: Generous and Asymmetric

**Spacing System** (based on 8px grid):
- xs: 8px (tight grouping)
- sm: 16px (related elements)
- md: 24px (section padding)
- lg: 48px (comfortable breathing room)
- xl: 64px (generous desktop spacing)
- 2xl: 96px (section breaks)

**Layout Strategy**:
- Single-column focus on mobile
- Asymmetric grid on desktop (not centered, intentional imbalance)
- Content max-width: 1280px (readable, not full-bleed)
- Generous margins: 10-15% on large screens

## Color Philosophy

### Color Approach

**Vibrant Monochrome**: Orange + Neutrals (as defined in [Color System Rules](../../.claude/rules/design/color-system.md))

**⚠️ IMPLEMENTATION NOTE**: When coding, use semantic tokens (e.g., `bg-page`, `bg-card`, `text-primary`, `bg-primary`) instead of hex values or Layer 1 core colors. See the [Color System Rules](../../.claude/rules/design/color-system.md) for the complete two-layer architecture.

**Reference Palette** (hex values shown for visual understanding):
- **Primary**: #FFAB00 (Vibrant Orange) → Use `bg-primary` or `text-accent` in code
- **Base**: #0A0A0A (True Black) → Use `bg-page` in code
- **Elevated**: #161A22 (Dark Gray) → Use `bg-card` in code
- **Text**: #F3F4F6 (Off-White) → Use `text-primary` for headings, `text-secondary` for body

**Color Psychology**:
- **Orange**: Energy, creativity, warmth, approachability, confidence
- **Black**: Sophistication, focus, modernity, letting work shine
- **White**: Clarity, professionalism, readability

**Why This Works**:
- High contrast = clear hierarchy
- Minimal palette = cohesive, not chaotic
- Orange stands out = strategic focus
- Dark background = OLED-friendly, modern, lets work pop

### Background Treatments

#### 1. **Solid Dark** (Primary)
```css
/* Reference hex: #0A0A0A */
background: var(--bg-page);  /* Use semantic token */
```
Tailwind: `bg-page`
Use for: Main page background, behind content sections

#### 2. **Subtle Mesh Gradient** (Hero Section)
```css
background: radial-gradient(circle at 30% 20%,
  rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.08) 0%,
  transparent 50%
);
```
Use for: Hero section background (orange glow, top right)

#### 3. **Elevated Cards**
```css
/* Reference hex: #161A22 (card), #1F2937 (border) */
background: var(--bg-card);
border: 1px solid var(--border-subtle);
```
Tailwind: `bg-card border border-subtle`
Use for: Project cards, content containers

#### 4. **Noise Texture Overlay** (Optional)
```css
background-image: url('/noise.png');
opacity: 0.03;
mix-blend-mode: overlay;
```
Use for: Adding warmth to pure black backgrounds

#### 5. **Animated Gradient** (Advanced, Optional)
```css
background: linear-gradient(
  135deg,
  #0A0A0A 0%,
  #1A0F0A 50%,
  #0A0A0A 100%
);
background-size: 200% 200%;
animation: gradientShift 15s ease infinite;
```
Use for: Hero section, adds subtle motion

## Typography Philosophy

**Complete system defined in**: [Typography System](./typography-system.md)

### Typography Approach

**Contrasting Duo**: Display Geometric + Neutral Sans

- **Headings**: Cabinet Grotesk (distinctive, condensed, personality)
- **Body**: Inter (invisible, readable, professional)
- **Code**: JetBrains Mono (technical credibility)
- **Signature**: Ms Madi (personal touch, footer only)

### Type as Visual Element

**Typography as Hero**: Large, bold headings (76px desktop) are the visual anchor. Not decoration—structure.

**Hierarchy Example** (Hero Section):
```
[Cabinet Grotesk, Bold, 76px, Orange]
Hey, I'm Jatin

[Inter, Regular, 24px, Gray]
Mobile Lead building cross-platform experiences
that feel smooth, scale well, and solve problems.

[Button: Inter, Semibold, 18px, Dark text on Orange BG]
View My Work
```

**Type Rhythm**:
- Headings: Tight line-height (1.05-1.2), tight tracking (-0.02em)
- Body: Comfortable line-height (1.6), neutral tracking
- Result: Clear contrast, easy scanning

### Kinetic Typography

**Subtle animation only**:
- Heading fade-in on page load (500ms)
- Letter-spacing shift on hover (interactive elements)
- NO: Character-by-character reveals, bouncing text, text rotations

## Imagery Style

### Photography Style

**Approach**: Minimal or None

**Personal Photos**:
- **Preferred**: Abstract/artistic representation (silhouette, partial, environmental)
- **Alternative**: Professional headshot (black & white or orange tint)
- **Location**: About section only
- **Style**: Dark background, high contrast, moody lighting

**NOT**: Casual selfies, stock photos, AI-generated faces

**If no photo**: Use abstract orange shape or single letter "J" as visual anchor

### Illustration Approach

**Minimal Line Illustrations (Sparingly)**:
- Simple geometric icons (arrow, code bracket, mobile phone outline)
- 2px stroke weight
- Orange accent color
- Used for section breaks or empty states

**NO**:
- Character illustrations (too playful)
- Isometric 3D (doesn't fit minimal aesthetic)
- Decorative patterns (too busy)
- Abstract shapes everywhere (visual noise)

### Icon Style

**Line Icons (Primary)**:
- 2px stroke weight
- 24px default size
- Color: Neutral-400 (default), Orange-500 (hover/active)
- Style: Geometric, minimal, clear

**Sources**:
- Lucide Icons (recommended)
- Heroicons
- Feather Icons

**Usage**:
- Navigation (hamburger menu)
- Social links (LinkedIn, Twitter, GitHub)
- Technology badges (code, mobile, web icons)

### Project Imagery

**Primary**: Clean product screenshots

**Best Practices**:
- 16:9 aspect ratio (consistent sizing)
- High resolution (2x for retina)
- Dark UI screenshots preferred (matches theme)
- Subtle mockups (device frames, minimal context)

**Image Treatments**:
```css
/* Standard project image */
border-radius: 8px;
overflow: hidden;

/* Hover effect */
transform: scale(1.05);
transition: transform 500ms ease-out;
```

**Project Image Types**:
1. **Hero shot**: Main feature, full-width
2. **UI close-ups**: Detailed interface shots
3. **Before/After**: Problem/solution comparisons (side-by-side)
4. **Video demos**: Embedded, autoplay on hover (muted)

**NO**:
- Busy mockup scenes (laptop + coffee + plant)
- Multiple devices in one shot (visual clutter)
- Stock photos pretending to be work
- Low-quality screenshots

## Special Effects & Modern Techniques

### Gradients

**Mesh Gradients (Background)**:
```css
/* Hero background glow */
background: radial-gradient(
  circle at 20% 30%,
  rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.1) 0%,
  rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.05) 30%,
  transparent 60%
);
```

**Gradient Text (Headings, Sparingly)**:
```css
.gradient-text {
  background: linear-gradient(
    135deg,
    #FFAB00 0%,
    #FF9500 100%
  );
  -webkit-background-clip: text;
  -webkit-text-fill-color: transparent;
}
```

**Animated Gradients (Buttons, Hover States)**:
```css
background: linear-gradient(
  135deg,
  #FFAB00 0%,
  #FF9500 100%
);
background-size: 200% 200%;
transition: background-position 300ms ease;

&:hover {
  background-position: 100% 100%;
}
```

**Usage Guidelines**:
- Background glows: Subtle (5-10% opacity)
- Text gradients: Sparingly (hero heading only)
- Animated gradients: Interactive elements only
- NO: Rainbow gradients, harsh color transitions, gradients everywhere

### Shadows & Glows

**NO Traditional Shadows** (don't show on dark backgrounds)

**YES Orange Glows**:
```css
/* Subtle glow (cards) */
box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.2);

/* Medium glow (buttons) */
box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.4);

/* Strong glow (hero elements) */
box-shadow: 0 0 40px rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.5);
```

**Glow Applications**:
- Button hover states
- Card hover effects
- Active navigation indicators
- Hero section decorative elements

**Inner Shadows** (Depth on cards):
```css
/* Subtle inset depth */
box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05);
```

### Glassmorphism

**Approach**: NO

**Why**: Glassmorphism (frosted glass effects) peaked in 2021-2023. By 2026, it's dated. Our aesthetic is cleaner, bolder.

**Alternative**: Solid elevated surfaces with subtle borders.

### 3D Elements

**Approach**: NO

**Why**:
- Adds complexity (performance, accessibility)
- Doesn't fit minimal aesthetic
- Risk of looking gimmicky

**Alternative**: Use 2D layering and orange glows to create depth.

**Exception**: If you want a subtle 3D touch, use CSS transforms only:
```css
/* Subtle card lift on hover */
transform: translateY(-4px);
```

### Blur Effects

**Background Blur (Headers, Modals)**:
```css
/* Sticky navigation */
background: rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.8);
backdrop-filter: blur(12px);
```

**Usage**: Sticky header, modal overlays

**NO**: Focal blur (depth of field), motion blur

### Grain/Noise

**Subtle Film Grain**: YES (adds warmth to digital darkness)

**Implementation**:
```css
/* Noise texture overlay */
.noise-overlay {
  position: fixed;
  top: 0;
  left: 0;
  width: 100%;
  height: 100%;
  background-image: url('/noise.png');
  opacity: 0.03;
  pointer-events: none;
  mix-blend-mode: overlay;
}
```

**Noise Texture**:
- 512x512px grayscale noise
- 3% opacity
- Overlay blend mode
- Adds analog warmth to pure digital

## Animation Philosophy

### Animation Approach

**Moderate and Purposeful**: Smooth transitions, rewarding micro-interactions, functional scroll animations.

**NOT**: Experimental physics, attention-grabbing effects, animations for animations' sake.

### Key Animation Moments

#### 1. **Hero Entrance** (Page Load)
```css
/* Fade up and in */
@keyframes fadeInUp {
  from {
    opacity: 0;
    transform: translateY(30px);
  }
  to {
    opacity: 1;
    transform: translateY(0);
  }
}

.hero-content {
  animation: fadeInUp 800ms ease-out;
}
```

**Sequence**:
1. Heading fades in (0ms delay)
2. Subtext fades in (200ms delay)
3. CTA button fades in (400ms delay)

#### 2. **Project Card Interactions**
```css
/* Hover state */
.project-card {
  transition: all 300ms ease-out;
}

.project-card:hover {
  transform: translateY(-4px);
  border-color: #FFAB00;
  box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.2);
}

/* Image zoom on hover */
.project-card:hover img {
  transform: scale(1.05);
  transition: transform 500ms ease-out;
}
```

#### 3. **Scroll-Triggered Reveals**
```css
/* Projects fade in as you scroll */
.project-card {
  opacity: 0;
  transform: translateY(30px);
  transition: all 600ms ease-out;
}

.project-card.in-view {
  opacity: 1;
  transform: translateY(0);
}
```

**Stagger**: Each card delays by 100ms

#### 4. **Navigation Transitions**
```css
/* Active link underline */
.nav-link::after {
  content: '';
  position: absolute;
  bottom: -4px;
  left: 0;
  width: 100%;
  height: 2px;
  background: #FFAB00;
  transform: scaleX(0);
  transition: transform 200ms ease-out;
}

.nav-link:hover::after,
.nav-link.active::after {
  transform: scaleX(1);
}
```

#### 5. **Button Interactions**
```css
/* Lift and glow on hover */
.button {
  transition: all 200ms ease-out;
}

.button:hover {
  transform: translateY(-2px);
  box-shadow: 0 0 30px rgba(255, 171, 0, 0.4);
}

.button:active {
  transform: translateY(0);
}
```

#### 6. **Loading States**
```css
/* Skeleton loader shimmer */
@keyframes shimmer {
  0% {
    background-position: -200% 0;
  }
  100% {
    background-position: 200% 0;
  }
}

.skeleton {
  background: linear-gradient(
    90deg,
    #161A22 0%,
    #1F2937 50%,
    #161A22 100%
  );
  background-size: 200% 100%;
  animation: shimmer 1.5s ease-in-out infinite;
}
```

#### 7. **Cursor Effects** (Optional, Advanced)

**Subtle Approach**:
```css
/* Custom cursor (orange dot follows) */
.cursor-dot {
  width: 8px;
  height: 8px;
  background: #FFAB00;
  border-radius: 50%;
  position: fixed;
  pointer-events: none;
  transition: transform 200ms ease-out;
}

/* Grows on interactive elements */
.button:hover ~ .cursor-dot {
  transform: scale(2);
}
```

**When to use**: Only on desktop, disable on mobile/tablets.


**Why**: Shows continuous learning without being gimmicky. Transparent about growth journey.

## 2025-2026 Portfolio Trends to Incorporate

Based on research from [Digital Synopsis Design Trends 2026](https://digitalsynopsis.com/design/graphic-design-trends-2026/) and [Wannathis Typography Trends](https://wannathis.one/blog/top-typography-trends-2026-for-designers):

### Layout Trends

#### 1. **Bento Box/Grid Layouts**
- Asymmetric grid of cards (different sizes)
- Use for: Skills section, project highlights
- Implementation: CSS Grid with varying column spans

#### 2. **Asymmetric Compositions**
- Intentionally unbalanced layouts
- Use for: Hero section (text left, visual right with offset)
- Creates visual interest without chaos

#### 3. **Sticky Scroll Reveals**
- Content appears/disappears as you scroll
- Use for: Project showcase (sticky project title, scrolling details)
- Smooth, informative, engaging

#### 4. **Full-Screen Sections with Snap Scroll**
- Each section is 100vh, snaps into place
- Use for: Hero, About, Projects, Contact
- Creates clear boundaries, focused attention

### Typography Trends

#### 1. **Extra Large Hero Text (76px+)**
- Oversized, unapologetic headings
- Already implemented: 76px desktop hero
- Establishes personality immediately

#### 2. **Variable Fonts with Subtle Animations**
- Cabinet Grotesk is a variable font
- Use for: Weight shifts on hover, subtle width changes
- Modern, performant, smooth

#### 3. **Mixed Type Sizes for Visual Interest**
- Different heading sizes in same section
- Example: "Hey, I'm [LARGE]Jatin[/LARGE]"
- Creates rhythm, breaks monotony

#### 4. **Elastic/Warped Typography** (Advanced, Optional)
- Typography that bends/stretches on interaction
- Use for: Hero heading on mouse move (subtle)
- Requires WebGL or complex CSS (optional enhancement)

### Interaction Trends

#### 1. **Magnetic Buttons**
- Buttons slightly move toward cursor
- Use for: Primary CTAs
- Implementation: Mouse move tracking + transform

#### 2. **Scroll Velocity Effects**
- Content moves at different speeds based on scroll speed
- Use for: Background elements
- Creates depth, dynamism

#### 3. **Micro-interactions Everywhere**
- Every hover, click, focus has subtle feedback
- Already planned: Button lifts, card glows, link underlines
- Reward every interaction

#### 4. **Physics-Based Animations** (Optional)
- Springy, natural motion (not linear)
- Use for: Modal open, drawer slide
- Implementation: Framer Motion library

### Visual Trends

#### 1. **Gradient Mesh Backgrounds**
- Complex, multi-point gradients
- Use for: Hero section (orange glow)
- Already planned: Radial gradient with orange

#### 2. **Dark Mode with Neon/Vibrant Accents**
- Pure dark + highly saturated accent
- Our approach: #0A0A0A + #FFAB00
- Perfect alignment with 2026 trends

#### 3. **Neo-Brutalism** (Avoid)
- Bold, flat colors, heavy borders
- Too aggressive for our "refined" brand
- Note: We're minimal, not brutal

#### 4. **Minimalism with Warmth**
- Clean layouts + natural textures
- Our approach: Dark minimal + orange warmth + subtle noise
- Exact alignment with [neo-minimalism trend](https://reallygooddesigns.com/graphic-design-trends-2026/)

## Do's and Don'ts

### Visual Do's ✅

1. **Use orange strategically** - CTAs, hero text, hover states, signature
2. **Embrace pure black** - #0A0A0A background, high contrast
3. **Let typography lead** - Large headings create structure
4. **Generous whitespace** - 48-64px padding on desktop
5. **Subtle animations** - 200-300ms, purposeful, smooth
6. **High contrast text** - #F5F5F5 on #0A0A0A (18.5:1 ratio)
7. **Show real work** - Actual screenshots, no stock photos
8. **Consistent borders** - 1px subtle, 2px on hover (orange)
9. **Respect dark theme** - Everything optimized for dark
10. **Glow, don't shadow** - Orange glows for depth on dark

### Visual Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't use orange everywhere** - It loses impact
2. **Don't use gray backgrounds** - Pure black or elevated dark only
3. **Don't add unnecessary 3D** - Flat/layered 2D is cleaner
4. **Don't animate everything** - Motion should be purposeful
5. **Don't use traditional shadows** - They don't show on dark
6. **Don't cram content** - If it feels tight, add more space
7. **Don't use busy mockups** - Clean product shots only
8. **Don't use colored shadows** - Orange glows only, not colored shadows everywhere
9. **Don't use multiple accent colors** - Orange only, no secondary colors
10. **Don't sacrifice readability** - If text is hard to read, fix contrast

### Content Do's ✅

1. **Show process, not just results** - How you solved problems
2. **Use specific numbers** - "34% conversion increase" not "improved performance"
3. **Let images breathe** - Don't crowd screenshots
4. **Write concisely** - Every word earns its place
5. **Use captions wisely** - Add context to images

### Content Don'ts ❌

1. **Don't use buzzwords** - No "synergy," "disrupt," "ninja"
2. **Don't inflate credentials** - Show, don't claim
3. **Don't hide your work** - Projects are the hero
4. **Don't use stock photos** - Real work only
5. **Don't write novels** - Scannable, concise, impactful
